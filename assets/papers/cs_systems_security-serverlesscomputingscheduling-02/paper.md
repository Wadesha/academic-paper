# Robust Serverless Computing Scheduling under Distribution Shift

> Companion Markdown. References are **real/verifiable** (OpenAlex/arXiv); experiments are **illustrative/simulated**.

## Abstract
This paper studies **Serverless Computing Scheduling**, grounded in a real, citable literature, and proposes a focused method with illustrative experiments.

## 1. Introduction
Serverless Computing Scheduling is an active research area. We target a concrete gap identified from recent literature.

## 3. Method
We model distribution shift explicitly and optimise a worst-case objective over an uncertainty set of plausible test distributions. We instantiate this idea for Serverless Computing Scheduling.
