## Abstract
This paper studies **Sea Level Rise Projection**, grounded in a real, citable literature, and proposes a focused method with illustrative experiments.

## 3. Method
We model distribution shift explicitly and optimise a worst-case objective over an uncertainty set of plausible test distributions. We instantiate this idea for Sea Level Rise Projection.
